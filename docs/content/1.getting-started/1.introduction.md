title: Introduction
description: What DiscordMute is and how its two parts work together.
---

DiscordMute keeps your Discord voice channel in sync with an Among Us round. It is made of two pieces you install together:

- **The plugin** (`DiscordMute.dll`) runs inside Among Us through BepInEx and reports lobby and game events.
- **The bot** is a Discord bot that mutes and unmutes people in the voice channel the lobby host sits in.

## What happens in a game

- During tasks everyone in the host's voice channel is muted.
- In meetings the living players are unmuted. Dead players stay muted.
- The bot unmutes the players it muted itself after a crash or restart.
